01 — PROJECT DOMAIN

Understanding the challenge.
Designing for resilience.

Explore the research context, the problem being addressed, the objectives, and the development approach behind Flood Guard 360.

01 · RESEARCH BACKGROUND
◉

Research Background

Flooding can disrupt homes, transport, essential services, and livelihoods. Effective response requires timely information, coordinated action, and continued support after floodwaters recede.

02 · PROBLEM STATEMENT
⌁

The Problem

Flood prediction, drainage information, relief distribution, and post-flood health support may be managed separately. Fragmented information can make coordinated decision-making harder.

03 · RESEARCH GAP
◇

Research Gap

There is a need for a joined-up approach that connects preparedness and prediction with operational drainage insights and post-disaster community recovery workflows.

04 · RESEARCH OBJECTIVES
✳

Project Objectives

  • Develop a flood-risk prediction and monitoring workflow.
  • Organise drainage information for monitoring and intervention.
  • Track post-flood needs and relief distribution.
  • Support illness awareness and disease-management activities.
  • Integrate the components and evaluate the system.
05 · METHODOLOGY

Research & Development Approach

Literature review → Data preparation → Model & module development → System integration → Testing & evaluation
